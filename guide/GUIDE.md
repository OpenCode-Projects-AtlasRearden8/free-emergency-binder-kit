# Where to Store Copies — A Short Guide

The printable version of this guide is `guide/guide.html` (built to
`dist/guide.pdf` by `build.py`). The short version:

1. **Build the binder** — print every PDF in `dist/`, add a 3-ring binder and
   page protectors, fill in the easy sheets by hand first, and do one slow
   house walk for the utility shutoffs and grab-and-go locations. About an hour.
2. **Store copies 3-2-1, family style:**
   - *Working copy* somewhere visible and findable in 30 seconds.
   - *Off-site copy* with your out-of-town contact person.
   - *Originals* (deeds, titles, birth certificates, passports) in a fire safe
     or locked box.
   - Optionally an encrypted scan on a drive you control.
3. **Tell two people** — one inside the home (where it is), one outside the
   home (that it exists), then set the Yearly Review reminder.

Privacy rules of thumb: nothing in this kit needs the internet, no accounts,
no uploads. Never store passwords in the binder, never write full account
numbers on the sheets, and keep ID scans encrypted if you digitize them.
