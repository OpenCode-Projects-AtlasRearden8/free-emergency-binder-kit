Emergency Binder Kit · The Guide

Where to Store Copies

Build the binder in one sitting (~1 hour). Store copies like the pros do: three places, no electronics required.

Step 1 — Build the Binder (one evening, coffee allowed)

  • Print every sheet in dist/ — hold onto extras of the Insurance One-Pager (one per policy).
  • Get a 3-ring binder, 8-ish page protectors, and a pencil.
  • Fill in the easy sheets first (contacts, household). Leave anything you're unsure about blank — blanks are fine, wrong info is not.
  • Do one slow walk of the house for the Utility Shutoff sheet and the Grab-and-Go locations.
  • Write the ICE letter last, when you know exactly who it's for.

Step 2 — Store Copies (3-2-1, family edition)

CopyWhere it livesWhy
Working copyA visible, known spot: kitchen shelf, entry closet, nightstandA binder nobody can find in 30 seconds is a decoration, not a tool.
Off-site copyThe out-of-town contact person keeps a photo or copyWorks even if the house is gone or unreachable.
Hidden originalsFire safe or locked box: deeds, titles, birth certificates, passports, ICE letterDocuments that are expensive or impossible to replace.
  • Optional fourth copy: encrypted scan on a drive or account you control.
  • Never: unencrypted scans of IDs on shared drives, passwords inside the binder, or full account numbers on this handwriting-friendly paper.

Step 3 — Tell Two People

  • Person inside the home: where the binder is, 10-second version.
  • Person outside the home: that it exists, and that they get the off-site copy.
  • Set the Yearly Review reminder — the project is complete.
Nothing in this kit connects to the internet or asks for an account. It prints, you write, it waits. That's it.
