In-Case-of-Emergency (ICE) Letter

A short letter to the person who steps in for you if you can't be there. Write it in handwriting, sign it, date it. Keep the original in the binder.

This letter is a template with blanks, not a legal document. For wills, powers of attorney, or medical directives, use a proper legal process — this letter simply tells someone where to start.

Letter Template (copy by hand or type, then sign)

Date: ______________________

Dear ______________________ ,

If you are reading this, something has happened and I need you to act for me. Thank you. Here is what matters most:

People: please contact ________________________________________ at ______________________ . They know the rest of the family and my wishes.

Pets: ______________ (name) should go to ______________________ . Feeding and vet info is on the Pet Info Cards in this binder.

Papers: important documents are kept ______________________ , and copies are in this binder / at ______________________ .

Money: household bills that must keep being paid are: ______________________ . Access to funds is through ______________________ (bank/contact) — details are with the documents, not in this letter.

Medical: my doctors are ______________________ . My preferences are written at ______________________ .

One thing I want you to know: ______________________________________________________

With thanks,

Signature: ________________________________

Notes to Yourself Before You Write It

  • Name the person most likely to step up first — then a backup. Keep money specifics out of the letter; point to where they are recorded.
  • The letter only works if the person knows the binder exists. Tell them. Today.
  • Re-read it yearly (Yearly Review card) — people and numbers change.
