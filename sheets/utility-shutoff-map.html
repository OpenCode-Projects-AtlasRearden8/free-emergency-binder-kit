Utility Shutoff Map & Instructions

Sheet 3 has the how-to; sheet 3B area below is your hand-drawn map. Show every adult and teen in the house where these are.

Gas Shutoff

  • Find the gas meter and the shutoff valve (a quarter-turn handle on the pipe coming out of the meter).
  • To close: turn the handle so it is crosswise (perpendicular) to the pipe.
  • Keep a wrench tied near the meter so it is always there.
  • Only the gas company restores gas. After shutoff, do not turn it back on yourself.
  • If you smell gas: leave first, call the gas company from outside or next door.

Water Shutoff

  • Main shutoff is usually where the water line enters the house (garage, basement, curb box near street).
  • Turn the valve clockwise until it stops. Ball handle = quarter turn.
  • After shutoff, open the lowest faucet in the house to drain pressure.
  • Burst-pipe rule: shutoff first, plumber second, panic never.

Electric Shutoff

  • Main breaker panel: know where it is; label the "MAIN" switch with tape.
  • Flip the MAIN to OFF before touching or cutting into anything electric.
  • Flood rule: electricity off before water touches outlets or appliances.
Nothing else in this binder matters more than one house walk. Physically locate each shutoff once, tag it with colored tape, draw it below. Then show a second adult. That is the whole project.
