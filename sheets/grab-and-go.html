Grab-and-Go Checklist

Three clocks: 5 minutes, 30 minutes, 60 minutes. In a real evacuation you rarely get to choose which one.

5 Minutes — People, Pets, Papers, Phone

  • Everyone accounted for (headcount first, always).
  • Pets + carriers / leashes.
  • This binder (it lives where you can grab it with one hand).
  • Wallet, keys, phone, chargers + power bank.
  • Medications in daily-use containers.
  • Shoes and one jacket per person — by habit, at the door.

30 Minutes — The "Go Bag" List

  • Water: 1 gallon per person per day, plus no-cook food and a manual can opener.
  • Flashlight + batteries; battery/hand-crank radio.
  • First-aid kit; 7-day supply of critical prescriptions.
  • Cash in small bills (ATMs and card readers fail together).
  • Printed photos of family, pets, and the house exterior.
  • Pet food + leash; litter bags; phone numbers copied onto paper.

60 Minutes — Add These

  • Laptops/external drives (or pull the drive, skip the laptop).
  • One change of clothes per person + sturdy shoes; toiletries.
  • Sleeping bags / blankets.
  • Tools: adjustable wrench (for utility shutoffs), work gloves, duct tape.
If you only remember one line: people, pets, papers, phone, pills. Everything else can be bought again. Nothing else is worth a life.
